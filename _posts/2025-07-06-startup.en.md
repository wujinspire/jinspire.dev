# 6 weeks into the startup! (Feb. 25 2025)

Recently felt the confusion and anxiety of entrepreneurs.

### About AI

Recently, my views on AI have changed again, which may be related to reading "The Evolution of Things".

Basically returned to the humanity school from the AI adventist school.

### Last year's AGI

Let's talk about AGI first. Last year I listened to many of Hinton's speeches, mostly about being vigilant against the catastrophe of AI. Hinton's speeches are very profound, very clear, without too much decoration, and very humorous.

Last year also started talking to ChatGPT every day, often starting to research a brand new field, including but not limited to psychology (how straight men pretend to have high F value), economics, philosophy, history, and the entire computer science. Indeed felt the power of AI. There were several nights holding the phone, asking questions and learning with ChatGPT, until 4 am.

### Recently

However, there have been changes recently, that is, I feel that the existence of AI is too low to have such high compensation. This sentence means that the precision of AI makes it very fragile, so perhaps before human extinction, AI has already perished. Humans are powerless to fight back against the catastrophe, but "lower" creatures can. So even if AI is so advanced, it is difficult to get rid of the laws of physics. When extreme situations occur, all AI will be destroyed instantly. The precision of AI makes it harder to replace humans to fly out of the solar system. So believe more that the answer to the Fermi paradox is so simple, that is the laws of physics, unable to make any civilization fill the universe. Universe seeding is the earliest civilization, and also the most powerless resistance to the laws of the universe.

So AI can think at the speed of light, and will also be destroyed at the speed of light.
